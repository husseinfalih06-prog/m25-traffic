# M25 traffic volume predictor (static pilot)

Predicted hourly traffic volumes at 20 National Highways counting sites around the M25 and London, for dates up to three months ahead.

Predictions are estimates from historical patterns in WebTRIS counts up to 30 June 2026. They are not live traffic and do not show congestion or journey times.

Contains National Highways WebTRIS data. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
